Before the negotiation

In the room: repeat these steps

  1. Hear the offer. Check what it includes. Say: "Let me check that for us."
  2. Enter it in the Calculator. Choose Their offer or Our offer. For ours, press Fill our next step.
  3. Read the checks. Look at the verdict at the top and What to check beside it.
  4. Press Send. The top strip says Testing, not sent, until you do. The Board follows the offer on screen as you test it.
  5. Read Our next move. Share it quietly with the team.
  6. Confirm the number before anyone says it. Take a team break before saying yes.

Rules that keep it safe

  • One person, one device. The Analyst enters everything. It saves only in that browser.
  • The ZOPA is partly a guess. Our hard line and Bent Anchor's limit are estimates.
  • Say precise figures. 38.25% or $24.70M, never "about 38" or "around 25 million".
  • Grey text is only an example. It is never counted. Starting figures are counted, so read Not updated yet before you send.
  • Extras show value to us only. We can't see what they cost Bent Anchor.
  • Leasing is not priced, and fuel savings start at zero. Agree them as a team if they come up.
